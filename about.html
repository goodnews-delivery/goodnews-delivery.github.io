Good News Delivery (좋은 소식 배달부) uploader

Good News Delivery is a one-person Korean channel that shares short, heart-warming domestic news stories every day on YouTube (@좋은소식배달부), Instagram and Threads (@good_news.delivery). This page describes the private tool the channel owner uses to publish.

What the tool does

  1. Each evening the owner's own computer prepares about 8 original YouTube Shorts (illustrations, captions written in our own words, credit to the original news outlet).
  2. The owner reviews and approves every video before anything is published.
  3. After approval, the tool uploads the approved Shorts to the owner's own channel with videos.insert, setting title, description (with source credit) and privacy status.
  4. Viewers who want the full story follow the profile link to our daily source page, which links to each original article.

YouTube data and scopes

  • Scopes: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly (to confirm the upload landed on the right channel).
  • Only the owner's own channel is accessed. No other users sign in, and no data of other users is collected, stored or shared.
  • The refresh token is stored only on the owner's computer.
